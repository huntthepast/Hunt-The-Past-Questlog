## Tabs

Permanent stat boosts. There is a fixed number of each in the game, so every one you skip is gone for that playthrough.

| Tab | Effect | Best on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Power Tab | +1 Power | Crono, Ayla |
| Magic Tab | +1 Magic | Lucca, Magus |
| Speed Tab | +1 Speed (max 16) | Your main attacker - Speed is the best stat in the game |

Early locations: **Guardia Forest (600 AD)** - Power Tab in the bushes; **Guardia Prison (1000 AD)** - Magic Tab in the second cell block. See [Part 1 of the walkthrough](/guides/chrono-trigger-walkthrough#part-1-guardia-forest-and-the-cathedral-600-ad) for the forest.

## Sealed chests

Blue glowing chests you cannot open until the Black Omen appears. The trick:

1. In **600 AD**, examine the chest but answer *no* when asked to open it.
2. In **1000 AD**, open the same chest for the *upgraded* item.
3. Go back to **600 AD** and open it again for the base item.

| Chest | 600 AD item | 1000 AD upgrade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Truce Inn | Blue Vest | Blue Mail |
| Porre Mayor's house | Black Vest | Black Mail |
| Guardia Castle (king's room) | Red Vest | Red Mail |

## Consumables worth keeping

- **Tonic / Mid Tonic** - cheap healing. Buy ten before every dungeon in the first act.
- **Ether** - MP restore. Rare early; save them for boss fights.
- **Shelter** - full heal at a save point. Two or three is plenty.
