## About this list

Names follow the US release, *Dragon Warrior*. In the Japanese original and the remakes, **Erdrick** is **Loto** and a few item names differ, but the stats and shops are the same.

**Price** is what the shops charge; **Sell** is what you get back. Hero Equipment, Erdrick's Sword and Erdrick's Armor, must be found and cannot be bought.

Click a column header to sort a table (by price, by attack...). Data from [gamercorner.net](https://guides.gamercorner.net/dw/arms/).

## Where the upgrades are

Read off the tables above, in order of the towns you reach:

- **Brecconary:** Bamboo Pole, Club, Copper Sword; Clothes, Leather Armor; Small Shield.
- **Garinham:** Club, Copper Sword, Hand Axe; Leather Armor, Chain Mail, Half Plate; Large Shield.
- **Kol:** Copper Sword, Hand Axe; Half Plate, Full Plate; Small Shield.
- **Rimuldar:** Copper Sword, Hand Axe, Broad Sword; Half Plate, Full Plate, Magic Armor.
- **Cantlin:** Bamboo Pole, Club, Copper Sword, Hand Axe, Broad Sword, Flame Sword; Leather Armor, Chain Mail, Full Plate, Magic Armor; Large Shield, Silver Shield.
- **Found, not bought:** Erdrick's Armor in Hauksness, Erdrick's Sword in Charlock Castle. Both sell for 1 G.
