## About these maps

The maps below were drawn by **Chiasm** and **Rick Bruns** and are hosted by
[Better VGMaps](https://vgmaps.de/maps/nes/dragon-warrior). They are their work, not mine, so this guide links to
each one rather than copying it - open a link and the full-size map loads on their site.

The gallery on this page is for maps I can host myself (my own screenshots, or ones I have permission for); it
groups them under **Overworld**, **Towns & castles** and **Dungeons**, the same three sections used below.

Names follow the US release, *Dragon Warrior*.

## Overworld

One map covers the whole world, which is all Dragon Warrior needs: every town, cave and shrine sits on it.

| Map | Drawn by | What is on it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Alefgard](https://vgmaps.de/maps/view?m=1938) | Rick Bruns | The entire overworld: the five towns, both castles, every cave entrance and the shrines. |

Useful to have open while you play - the buried Erdrick's Token sits in the desert south-east of Tantegel, and
the Rainbow Drop bridge appears on the west shore of Charlock's island.

## Route through the caves

1. **Erdrick's Cave** as soon as you can fight - read the tablet, then come back for the Token later.
2. **Swamp Cave** once you have the Broad Sword: rescue Gwaelin from the Green Dragon.
3. **Mountain Cave** for the Death Necklace, which pays for most of your next upgrade.
4. **Garin's Grave** for the Silver Harp, then the **Magic Temple** to trade it for the Staff of Rain.
5. **Hauksness** for Erdrick's Armor, then the Sunstone shrine and **Charlock Castle** with the Rainbow Drop.
