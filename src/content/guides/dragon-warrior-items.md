## About this list

Names follow the US release, *Dragon Warrior*. **Price** is what the shops charge, **Sell** is what you get back for it.

Your bag holds eight slots. Herbs, Magic Keys and Torches stack up to six per slot; everything else takes a slot of its own, so sell or use the spares before a long trip.

Click a column header to sort a table. Data from [gamercorner.net](https://guides.gamercorner.net/dw/items/).

## Cursed items

Worth real gold at a shop, worthless the moment you equip them: a curse locks you out of Tantegel Castle until an old man in the north-west of Rimuldar removes it. Sell them, never wear them.

| Name | Sell | Where to find | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cursed Belt | 180 G | Garinham, Charlock Castle | Cursed - blocks entry to Tantegel Castle. |
| Death Necklace | 1,200 G | Mountain Cave | Cursed - blocks entry to Tantegel Castle. The most valuable thing you can sell. |

## What to carry

- **Always:** a few Herbs, one or two Magic Keys, and Torches before any cave (the Radiant spell replaces them once you learn it).
- **Early game:** the Dragon's Scale is 20 G for a permanent +2 Defense - buy it on the first trip.
- **Before Charlock:** Fairy Water helps you walk past the weaker encounters, and Wings save the walk home.
- **Sell on sight:** Fighter's Ring, Cursed Belt and especially the Death Necklace (1,200 G funds most of the Flame Sword).
